# Factory Worker Monotonic Intent Refinement

Only a Factory Worker may refine an **existing** owning `IDD-NNNN` spec,
Living ADR or active spike during its assigned implementation or research
Task. The edit must preserve the effective approved product contract, have
enduring value and respect explicit user restrictions. This authority does not
extend to ordinary `idd-code-implement` or any other skill.

Discover the owner through the Intent README, INDEX and relevant
`TaskRelatedIntent` IDs. Read additional owners selectively when necessary,
within the Task scope and without reselecting Conditional Engineering Rules.
Read the bounded Factory baseline and preflight/Worker provenance, or the
explicitly supplied verified contract boundary. Unattributed edits do not
establish approved obligations.

For each proposed edit verify all of the following:

1. The document is the existing authoritative `IDD-NNNN` owner.
2. The edit follows from approved requirements or reproducible technical
   evidence. State the conditions and limits of that evidence.
3. It preserves normative behavior, guarantees, scope, accepted architecture,
   exceptions, non-goals and Acceptance Criteria.
4. It provides durable value to a future Worker.

Lack of contradiction alone is not proof. Record confirmed experiments,
relevant rationale and technical clarifications that preserve meaning. A
verified fact does not create a product promise or Engineering policy. Leave
`.idd/engineering/` unchanged. Do not create normative owners or parallel
normative specifications under `doc/` or `docs/`, independently edit
`GLOSSARY.md`, invent an ADR, normalize all Intent or replace an approved
requirement to match implementation. Accompany authorized edits with mechanical
INDEX or link updates only when needed. Preserve external original references
under `doc/` and `docs/` and retain their source names when possible.

If evidence, ownership, provenance, authority or monotonicity is ambiguous,
leave the disputed text untouched and report the finding or blocker for the
owner workflow. Avoid duplicating knowledge after interrupted attempts. After
an edit check semantic equivalence, structure, links and duplication.
