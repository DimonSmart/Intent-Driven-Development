# COSMIC analysis and optional scaling

Use the simplest workflow that preserves complete accessible source coverage and traceable owner-process estimates. The methodology and assumptions are defined in the measurement guide. Native child-agent isolation, persistent cache and hierarchical reduction are **optional optimizations**, not applicability requirements.

## Small or manageable scope

Read each selected spec fully or reuse an accepted complete extraction. In the same context, identify owned processes, LINK connectors and E/X/R/W; distinguish explicit, implied and assumed movements in Approximate mode. Keep a compact per-source inventory and owner-process table, aggregate functional areas and reconcile counts. No forced separate reader/reducer/measurer passes. If no agents or cache are available, use sequential/grouped reads.

## Larger scope

Partition by functional area, owner or bounded document group. Use native child agents when useful/available, or process bounded chunks sequentially while retaining a compact inventory and accessible evidence. Very large sources can be read in source ranges and consolidated; do not treat headings or chunks as independent processes. Track coverage, no lost input or duplicate owner keys. If a host limit actually prevents completion, report the assessed subtotal, exact unprocessed paths and concrete limitation.

- Reader/extractor: source-backed process/event/actor facts, candidate movements, links and gaps, with exact source anchors. Cache only extraction, not final estimates or assumed requirements.
- Analyzer: apply the requested boundary/mode to source facts; produce per-process E/X/R/W identities, provenance (Explicit/Implied/Assumed), central CFP, optional justified bounds, area and material assumptions. May be the reader itself; no mandatory extra agent. Do not rely on cached previous CFP.
- Reducer/coordinator: reconcile every requested source, owner keys and cross-group links; keep dependencies separate from requested owners. Merge area totals, not overlapping boundaries. Preserve detailed evidence and assumptions in accessible context/packets; do not repeat all internal bookkeeping in the report. Recount and check arithmetic.

Keep handoffs within usable context with room for instructions, reasoning and output. Use fresh bounded groups or additional reduction only when necessary. For explicit requests for hierarchical analysis, honor available capabilities; if unavailable, disclose and fall back without claiming isolation.

## Ownership and coupling

One canonical owner per process, one count. Other specs reference that owner through LINK. Unresolved links are noted when materially relevant; never become independent processes or add movement counts. Genuine duplicate normative definitions are reported to structure audit, not silently resolved. A caller's own independent functional response may have its own justified movements, but a repeated internal invocation is not a movement by itself.

On request, report separate non-COSMIC coupling: distinct confirmed caller-process fan-in, referring-spec count when caller identity is unknown, outbound links and qualitative change risks. Do not equate mentions with callers or multiply CFP/hours by fan-in. The qualitative business complexity skill is the primary place for change-impact analysis.

## Extraction packets

If using persistent cache, follow the numbered Markdown contract in the execution-state guide. A packet is a source snapshot, not a finished size or effort result. Reinterpret the facts for the current request; do not turn an assumption into normative evidence. Use any available native read/analysis method without requiring a custom execution runtime or validator.
